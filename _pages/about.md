<p>I work at the intersection of computational modeling, material performance, and manufacturing processes. My work involves building physics-based models to understand, predict, and optimize thermo-mechanical behavior in polymer and composite systems. I develop validated simulation frameworks, perform targeted experiments, and deliver practical tools for process and product improvement.</p>

<strong>Core Expertise:</strong>
- Multi-physics modeling (thermo-mechanical, cure/viscoelasticity, melt behavior)
- Finite Element Modeling (ABAQUS), MATLAB, Python
- Process development for polymer/composite manufacturing
- Experimental characterization (rheology, DSC/DMA, microscopy, tensile testing)
- Model calibration and experimental validation

Outside work, I enjoy gardening, hiking, and reading; currently reading *Crime and Punishment*.
