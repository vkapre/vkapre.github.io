<p>Engineering researcher focusing on physics-based modeling and simulation of fiber-reinforced polymer composites to predict and optimize manufacturing performance.</p>

<strong>Current:</strong> Postdoctoral Researcher, Mechanical Engineering, Purdue University — Additive Manufacturing of Fiber-Reinforced Polymer Composites.  
<strong>Target Roles:</strong> Modeling & Simulation Engineer or Manufacturing/Process R&D in Aerospace and Automotive; open to other sectors.

<strong>Core Expertise:</strong>
- Modeling & simulation: DEM, FEM, transient heat transfer, and multiphysics coupling  
- Process engineering: extrusion, additive manufacturing, solid–melt transitions  
- Programming & workflows: Python, LIGGGHTS, ABAQUS, MATLAB, automated validation pipelines  
- Material characterization: microstructural, thermal, mechanical, and viscoelastic properties of orthotropic material systems  

Outside work, I enjoy gardening, hiking, and reading; currently reading *Crime and Punishment*.
